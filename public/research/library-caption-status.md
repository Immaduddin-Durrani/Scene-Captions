# Film caption coverage

All three films are full-length local demos with their own credits. Nothing is generated while viewing.

| Film | Available captions | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Big Buck Bunny | 87 licensed non-speech source intervals, with source-anchored wording variants | Full listening/readability review before a study remains pending |
| Sintel | 26 English dialogue subtitle cues extracted from the official 720p MKV | No verified non-speech track or approved personalized sound variants; official subtitle coverage does not include all sounds or end-credit lyrics |
| Tears of Steel | 76 English subtitle cues from the official Blender download directory | No verified non-speech track or approved personalized sound variants |

The new titles preserve source wording and timestamps. These are dialogue subtitles, not complete captions for Deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers. Their sound preferences are disabled. A full sound-caption track needs genuine listening/wording review; none was invented or marked approved. The previous failed model gate remains in force.

Changing films resets playback, temporary preview and original override, then loads that film’s own subtitle data and credits. Saved viewing preferences remain in the browser. Rehearsal fixes the selected media until reload; only a sound-caption-ready track can start the current rehearsal.

Sources: [Sintel official film/archive](https://download.blender.org/durian/movies/), [Tears of Steel English subtitles](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/subtitles/TOS-en.srt).
