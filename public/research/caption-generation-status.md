# SceneCaptions caption status

Updated 24 October 2026.

The working demo uses 87 licensed sound-caption intervals from the Demuxed Big Buck Bunny track. Original wording and timing are preserved. Personalized wording is prepared offline; no model runs while you watch.

Follow cue density can shorten captions in busy cue passages. It never increases detail above your chosen maximum. This is a density proxy, not verified visual scene understanding.

The temporary preview tries your draft settings for five seconds without saving them. You can return to saved settings immediately or use Show original.

Automatic model preparation has not passed its quality checks. Text trials copied source wording, omitted sounds or returned unsuitable output. Audio transport and a speech control worked, but a known tone was classified as silence. Model-generated candidates have not replaced the working captions.

Some source intervals and wording still need a full listening and reading-time review. A shorter caption is not automatically accurate. No participant results or accessibility benefits have been established.
