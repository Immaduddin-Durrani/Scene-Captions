## Offline preparation and acceptance gates

New tools live outside the web runtime: `scripts/generate-caption-variants.py`, `scripts/check-caption-audio.py`, `scripts/generation/`, and `scripts/diagnostics/`. They use the existing private key through either supported variable name. The key file was not rewritten.

Generation validates the source track, works on individual sound labels, combines simultaneous sounds deterministically, caches responses and writes only new candidate files. It rejects missing/wrong IDs, incomplete grids, lost/added sound lines, excessive length and source-copy-only batches. None of these structural checks proves semantic accuracy. An early structurally valid probe omitted a simultaneous sound; another probe only echoed sources. These were rejected and quarantined under ignored artifacts. No model candidate replaced the working track.

Corresponding audio is extracted from the exact-hash movie and source intervals. The audio-observation prompt withholds the expected source labels. A failed calibration prevents model-assisted matching; extracted clips can still support human listening review. No existing failed capability gate was cleared.

Reading flags use a 20-character/second authoring heuristic, not money/token limits or a validated DHH threshold. The current track has 206 original/variant review flags. Some source originals are also short/long-text cases. Timings are never automatically extended, and shorter wording is not proof of accuracy.

## Model results and causal limits

### Initial probes

- Lightning authenticated and returned usable basic text/JSON, but caption trials copied the source, omitted a simultaneous sound or produced an incomplete grid. A full run stopped on invalid output; another was stopped after repeated source copies rather than continued through the film.
- Bolt returned an unrelated breeze while rewriting a music caption. GPT OSS produced no usable result in its bounded trial; Gemma timed out. These are test outcomes, not general rankings of the underlying model families.
- The structured audio probe returned the example-like sound entry on digital silence. This failed the gate. No film inference proceeded through that failed gate.

### Controlled diagnosis

Seven initial controls used only synthetic material and simple text, followed by two positive audio controls. See the [evidence index](../verification/fixes-2026-10-24/README.md).

| Control | Result | What this establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple arithmetic | `25` for 17 + 8 | Authentication/basic text completion works. |
| Broad sound type from a serene-music caption, reasoning off/on | `ambient` / `Ambient` | Reasoning toggle did not preserve the required music type in this test. |
| Digital silence, plain classification | SILENCE | Simpler prompting avoids the earlier example echo, but this alone does not establish audio understanding. |
| Known audible tone, reasoning off/on | SILENCE both times | The tested setup fails an elementary non-speech distinction. |
| Invalid WAV bytes in the same audio field | Decoder error, HTTP 500 | The endpoint processes/decodes the audio field; it is not simply ignored at the transport layer. |
| Locally synthesized known speech | Correct transcript apart from joining “blue bird” | Valid audio reaches a speech-capable processing path. No human recording was transmitted. |

Inference: evidence points toward a non-speech recognition/task weakness in the tested hosted setup rather than absent film context or completely broken audio transport. It does not isolate weights vs serving configuration or establish failure on every possible sound. Prompt sensitivity also exists. Correct speech transcription is not proof of sound-effect recognition.

## Remaining work

The user's 40-RPM constraint is configured with shared local pacing at about 29 RPM. Temporary responses/transports retry at most six times after the initial attempt, with 2/4/8/16/32/60-second waits. Retry-After is honored within that ceiling; longer provider waits stop the run. Normal/reasoning response timeouts default to 180/300 seconds and can be overridden explicitly. These are operational controls, not evidence that prior model-quality failures are fixed. Earlier 30–60 second diagnostic timeouts are recorded as inconclusive outcomes, not capability failures.

Automatic non-speech corroboration and acceptable AI wording generation remain unproven. Keep the working source-caption demo; evaluate a replacement only against the same small controls before any full-film run. Scene/script notes can support annotation, but must not become expected answers in an allegedly independent audio test. Full caption listening/readability review, DHH feedback, final stimulus/questions, ethics/instructor approval and formal study readiness remain pending. Component maintainability is a documented follow-up rather than a reason to destroy the working codebase.
